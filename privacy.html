1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically when you use our website:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any other details you submit when you contact us or create an account.
  • Account Credentials: Username and password when you register for access to our modules. Passwords are stored securely and never in plain text.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, and interactions with our features.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and device type.
  • Cookies: Small files stored on your device that help the website function (see Cookies & Tracking).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and services
  • Create and manage your account, including authentication and access control
  • Respond to your inquiries, requests, and support questions
  • Process contact form submissions and communicate with you
  • Send important notices about changes to our services or policies
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve performance, usability, and content
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on one or more of the following:

  • Consent: When you voluntarily provide your information or agree to specific processing.
  • Contract: When processing is necessary to provide a service you have requested or registered for.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate, secure, and improve our website in ways you would reasonably expect.
  • Legal Obligations: When required to comply with applicable law.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • With service providers who assist us in operating the website, such as hosting and email services (see Third-Party Services)
  • To comply with a legal obligation, court order, or government request
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, or that of our users
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you

5. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored on Google's secure cloud infrastructure via Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When we no longer need your data, we securely delete or anonymize it.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information, including:

  • Encrypted transmission of data (HTTPS)
  • Hashed storage of passwords
  • Access controls restricting who can view or edit data
  • Restricted access to backend systems and databases

While we work hard to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to keep the website functional, such as remembering your login session and theme preferences. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking purposes.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of certain features.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our website:

  • Google Apps Script & Google Sheets: Contact form submissions, account management, and data storage.
  • Cloudflare Pages: Website hosting and content delivery.
  • Cloudflare Analytics: Aggregated, privacy-friendly usage statistics.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and post the updated policy here. If changes are significant, we may provide additional notice. Continued use of our website after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out to us through the Contact page.

We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 30 days.
